Depth Sensing — Time-of-Flight vs Structured Light vs Stereo

One synthetic 3D scene, ray-cast through three different depth-sensing architectures at once, so their real tradeoffs — range, ambient-light sensitivity, and surface dependence — show up as something you can watch happen rather than read about. A pedagogical model, not a physically exact simulator — see README.md.
