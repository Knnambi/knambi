IMU Sensing — accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and fusion

One rigid body, moved by a preset motion profile or your own keyboard input, sensed by a simulated accelerometer/gyroscope/magnetometer with real noise (white noise, bias, bias drift) — then reconstructed three ways (gyro-only, complementary filter, Kalman filter) so drift and correction are visible directly. A pedagogical model, not a physically exact simulator — see README.md.

Motion

Manual: W/S forward/back, A/D strafe, R/F up/down, arrow keys pitch/yaw, Q/E roll.
